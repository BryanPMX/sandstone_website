## Can I Buy a House With No Down Payment?

If you've been dreaming of homeownership but haven't saved enough for a traditional down payment, you're not alone — and you're not out of options. The good news is that **buying a house with no down payment is entirely possible** through specific loan programs designed for eligible buyers.

---

## 1. Yes, You Can — With the Right Loan Program

Several government-backed mortgage programs allow qualified buyers to purchase a home with 0% down. These programs were created specifically to remove the biggest barrier to homeownership: the upfront cost. If you meet the eligibility requirements, you could move into your new home without putting a single dollar down.

---

## 2. VA Loans: The Best Zero-Down Option for Veterans

If you are an active-duty service member, veteran, or eligible surviving spouse, a **VA loan** is one of the strongest no-down-payment mortgage options available.

**Key benefits of VA loans:**
- 0% down payment required
- No private mortgage insurance (PMI)
- Competitive interest rates
- Flexible qualifying guidelines

VA loans are backed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and can provide an affordable path to homeownership for eligible borrowers.

---

## 3. USDA Loans: Zero Down for Rural and Suburban Homes

The **USDA Rural Development loan** allows eligible buyers in qualifying rural and suburban areas to finance 100% of a home's purchase price with no down payment.

**Who may qualify for a USDA loan?**
- Buyers purchasing in a USDA-eligible area
- Households that meet applicable income limits
- Buyers who meet the lender's credit and underwriting requirements

Use the USDA's official property eligibility tools to check whether a home may qualify.

---

## 4. Down Payment Assistance Programs Can Help With Upfront Costs

Even if you don't qualify for a VA or USDA loan, you may still be able to reduce your upfront costs through **down payment assistance programs**.

Depending on the program, assistance may come in the form of:

- Grants
- Forgivable loans
- Deferred-payment loans
- Other local or state assistance programs

These programs may be available to first-time homebuyers and other eligible buyers depending on income, location, and program requirements.

---

## 5. What to Consider Before Buying With No Down Payment

While buying with no down payment can be a valuable option, it's important to understand the potential trade-offs:

- **Higher monthly payments** — financing more of the purchase price can increase your monthly payment
- **More interest paid over time** — a larger loan balance can result in more total interest
- **Less immediate equity** — you may begin with little or no equity in the property
- **Additional program costs** — some loan programs include mortgage insurance, funding fees, or other requirements

For many buyers, however, a low- or no-down-payment program may make homeownership possible sooner.
